EasyFood on your iPhone

Set up in about 2 minutes. No App Store needed.

EasyFood is our shared meal planner. Once you've joined the household, we have the same recipes, meal plan and grocery list, and anything one of us changes shows up on the other's phone within seconds.

Setting it up

  1. Open the link you got in the message in Safari.

    If it opened inside WhatsApp or another app, tap the compass or Open in Safari first. Only Safari can add apps to the Home Screen.

  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.

    Don't see it? Tap ··· next to the address bar first, then Share.

  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen , then Add. (If there's an Open as Web App switch, leave it on.)
  4. Close Safari and open EasyFood from your Home Screen.

    Always use this icon from now on, not the Safari tab. The two keep separate data.

  5. Got an invite to a household? The app asks "Join this household?". Tap Join. Done! Just got the app link? You're already done, and everything you add stays on your phone.

    Not asked? Tap the gear at the top of the Home tab to open Settings. Under Household, type the code from the message (like ABCD-EFGH-JKMN) and tap Join.

Make it yours

In Settings → Nutrition profiles, add a profile for yourself with your own daily targets. Profiles, water and weight are personal and stay on your phone. Everything else is shared:

  • Recipes (also ones you add or edit)
  • The meal planner
  • The grocery list. Tick things off in the shop and it updates on the other phone too.
  • Pantry and freezer

Good to know

No internet?
The app keeps working. Your changes are sent as soon as you're back online.
Updates
The app updates itself. A new version is used from the next time you open it.
Scanning barcodes
Allow camera access the first time you scan. The first scan needs internet to load the scanner.
Recipes from websites
Most recipe sites can't be imported on iPhone. Ask the Android phone to import it, and it shows up for you too.
Reminders
The freezer and expiry reminders only work in the Android app. On iPhone you won't get those notifications.
"Couldn't sync"?
Check your internet, then go to Settings → Household → Sync now.
Deleted the app by accident?
Nothing is lost. The household keeps everything. Follow the steps above again and join with the same code.
